Questions, answered

The essentials.

Have a question that is not here? Reach support@Jewellink.com.

What is JewelHire?+

JewelHire is a recruiting, assessment, and onboarding platform created specifically for jewelry stores. It helps owners create a year-round hiring system, not just fill an urgent opening.

What is Jewel Cert™?+

Jewel Cert™ is a candidate-readiness report based on aptitude assessments, product-knowledge checks, and assigned training. It gives hiring teams better context before an interview.

What is Gem Match™?+

Gem Match™ is a jewelry-specific team-fit assessment for current associates and applicants. It helps owners understand working styles, role alignment, and possible team complement.

Can candidates apply through my own website?+

Yes. JewelHire gives your store a careers page and application flow that can be linked from your website, job posts, QR codes, or event materials.
